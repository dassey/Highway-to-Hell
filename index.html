The data

Crash records come from NHTSA's Fatality Analysis Reporting System (FARS) — the federal census of every motor-vehicle crash on a public road that kills someone within 30 days. Roads are grouped by the trafficway identifier (TWAY_ID) states report, so the same highway can appear under more than one name, and a road named in two states counts each state's stretch. — crashes with unusable coordinates are not shown. Data is public domain; this site is not affiliated with NHTSA or USDOT.

Your corner of the map

Search a town or address (or hit the crosshair for your location) to drop a pin and draw a 1–10 mile ring — the numbers then count only what happened inside it. Drag the pin to move it. Place lookups go to the open Photon geocoder; your device location is only used to move the map and never leaves your browser.
